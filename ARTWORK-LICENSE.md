# Art, text, and type

**Celestial Arcana, card backs, observatory background, and teaching text in all three languages.** The Arcana Observatory contributors offer these works under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). When sharing or adapting them, credit Arcana Observatory contributors, link to the license, and identify modifications. The Celestial Arcana illustrations and the observatory's planetary landscape were created with ImageGen.

**Rider–Waite–Smith.** Pamela Colman Smith illustrated the historical deck. The app uses the Pam-A scan set uploaded by TaionWC. Each card's catalog entry records its source. [Wikimedia Commons collection](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Rider-Waite-Smith_tarot_deck_(TaionWC))

**Tarot de Marseille.** Jean-Pierre Payen, Avignon. The historical woodcuts were digitized from the Cary Collection of Playing Cards, Beinecke Rare Book and Manuscript Library, Yale University Library, FRA166. Each card's catalog entry identifies its source and crop. [Yale record](https://collections.library.yale.edu/catalog/32296393) · [Yale open-access terms](https://web.library.yale.edu/about/copyright/permission.html)

**Type.** Rubik is distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The same variable font supplies Latin, Hebrew and Cyrillic lettering. [Font license and copyright](assets/fonts/rubik-OFL.txt) · [Google Fonts source revision](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/5026800714d07231937d92eb4cceabf06e05d74e/ofl/rubik)
